Chapter 01 · Connect

Connect anything with gold threads.

Link verse to verse and story to testimony with living gold threads. Build mind maps, timelines and flowcharts where cross-references run for miles, a web of understanding only you could map.

Every note is joined to the thoughts around it, and the threads follow when you move it.

Lasso, duplicate and move anywhere.
