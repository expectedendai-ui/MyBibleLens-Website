Scripture Canvas

An endless canvas for the Word.

Bible study is not a straight line. On Scripture Canvas a verse sits next to a note, a photo, a sticker and the verse it reminds you of, with room to keep going in every direction.

  • VerseType the reference and the words from your Bible, and stamp a Scripture card.
  • NoteTap Note, or double-tap empty space, and write. Some words come alive with their own art.
  • ConnectJoin cards with connectors that stay attached as you move your ideas around.
  • Pencil, Highlight, NeonWrite clean lines, lay down see-through colour, or glow with the LED pen.
  • LassoCircle a whole idea and move it as one, or keep it in your private Lasso Vault.
  • Sacred Layouts46 ready-made studies: verse ladders, timelines, maps and more.
  • Stickers and landscapes1,119 original stickers in seven art styles and 68 landscapes to set the scene.
  • Pin, Media, PresentDrop lettered markers, add your photos and present your study without the toolbars.

Home

Home is for your people.

Scripture Canvas is just you. Home is together: everything that involves the people you study with lives in one place.

  • Waiting for you. Friend requests and canvas invitations, ready to accept.
  • Start a shared canvas. Name it and pick friends or a group.
  • Your fellowship. The friends you added with their Kingdom ID. No directory, no public profile.
  • Groups. Family, Bible Study or Church. Church groups with a pastor are coming soon.

Shared canvases open in an update. Until then, a canvas you start from Home stays yours, and your friends and groups are ready for the day sharing opens.

    Honest Answers

    The questions friends ask before they trust us with their faith practice.

    What is MyBibleLens? +

    MyBibleLens is a sanctuary app for Christianity built around Scripture Canvas, an endless canvas for the Word. You stamp verses, write notes, connect ideas and decorate a study with stickers and landscapes. Journey gives you a calm reading plan, and Home keeps your friends and groups close.

    Does the app have the Bible in it? +

    No. You read in your own Bible, the one in your hands. On a canvas you type the reference and the words you want to keep, and in Journey you tap I read it when you finish a day.

    Can I make a canvas with my church or small group? +

    Soon. Today you can add friends by their Kingdom ID, make groups and start a shared canvas from Home. Sharing opens in an update; until then that canvas stays yours. There are no public links and nothing is posted where strangers can find it.

    What happens if I lose my phone or get a new one? +

    Sign in on the new device and your canvases and your Journey come back. They are saved to your account and stay in sync across your iPhone and iPad.

    Is this for a specific denomination? +

    MyBibleLens is built for anyone who reads the Bible. It does not lean into any one denomination's distinctives. You'll find believers from Catholic, Protestant, Orthodox, and non-denominational backgrounds using it side by side. If a feature ever pushes a particular tradition, that's a bug. Tell us at info@mybiblelens.us.

    How is my data protected? +

    Your canvases are private by default. Supabase Row-Level Security enforces this at the database level, and we never sell or advertise against your spiritual content. Read the full Privacy Policy and AI Disclosure for details.

    Where can I get the app? +

    MyBibleLens is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. We are rebuilding it around Scripture Canvas right now, so some things may change while we finish. The Android version is in the works. Follow @mybiblelens on Instagram to be the first to know.
